# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added
- New interface, Home: a hero card at the top (design board C): the song that plays now with its cover's colour glowing behind it and a Pause / Play button, or the paused song. If Xplayer was closed in the middle of a song, Home shows that song as "Pick up where you left off", with "Resume from 1:02" and "Start over" (it never starts playing by itself). Under it, a grid of up to 8 small tiles (the playlists and albums you played last, one click to open). The shelves have their own shapes: wide playlist cards, round centred artists. The app now remembers the song and its second (about every 5 seconds, on pause and on close) under one setting key.
- `tools/dev/finder-report.js`: a read-only report that runs the multi tag finder on a spread of your songs and shows what the "original" and "most popular" rules pick, with confidence and reasons.
- New interface, visualizer: a "Visualizer width" setting (Settings > Playback > Player bar): Full width, or Left side, a strip from the left edge to just before the transport controls (30 to 40 % of the player section). The song info now comes back instantly when the pointer returns and still slides away with an animation; and a mouse click on a player button no longer keeps it open (only keyboard focus does).
- New interface: an audio visualizer. Bars rise from the bottom edge of the player and follow the music, behind the controls; the song info slides away to the left while they play and the pointer is elsewhere. Off by default: switch it in Settings > Playback > Player bar, or with the bars button in the player. Drawn at 30 frames per second and at screen scale 1 at most, and only while a song plays. `node tools/dev/bench-visualizer-app.js` measures what it costs in the real app.
- New interface, smoother seek bar: the plain bar and the waveform now move every frame between the player's time reports instead of stepping about four times a second, and the waveform's played part ends at the exact pixel instead of at the nearest bar. With less animation turned on they step with the player's reports, as before.
- New interface, dragging: a song or Windows files held over a rail item (Library, Home, Lyrics, Settings ...) for about half a second opens that item, shown with a ring while it waits, so a song can be carried to a playlist in the left panel in one drag. An item that is already open is left as it is.
- New interface: the Back and Forward buttons in the title bar now walk every place you were (Home, Lyrics, Metadata, Settings, the AI chat and the library pages), the same history the mouse side buttons use, and they are greyed out when there is nowhere to go. Before, they knew only the library pages.
- Dragging audio files in from Windows: the left panel dims what cannot take them (only Liked Songs, playlists and All songs stay lit), a file dropped on a playlist or on Liked Songs is imported and put there, a drop on All songs imports it, and the queue drawer opens while the files rest above its button so they can be dropped into the queue (they go after the songs added by hand). A drop anywhere else does nothing, and the app no longer tries to open a file dropped outside the list. One shared rule (`ui/06l-drop-rules.js`) now decides what can take a drop, for song drags and file drags alike.
- New interface, dragging: the queue drawer now also opens when a dragged song rests for a moment in a zone above the queue button (on the right, where the drawer appears). The zone is taller for a longer queue. Dragged song rows are dimmed during the drag, as dragged left-panel items already were. Covers and other pictures can no longer be dragged out of the window.
- Tags in M4A / MP4 files can be read and edited in the Metadata editor (title, artists, album, genre, year, track and disc, comment, BPM, sort names, MusicBrainz ids and more). Before, only the cover could be changed, and only with Python installed.
- `npm run bench:gpu` measures scrolling and the waveform with hardware acceleration off and on; `XPLAYER_GPU=on` turns it on for one run. The default (off) is unchanged.
- Electron fuses in the built exe (`package.json` > `build.electronFuses`): `NODE_OPTIONS` and `--inspect` are turned off. The build job checks them with `tools/dev/check-fuses.js`.
- A Windows build job on GitHub (`.github/workflows/build.yml`) builds the portable app and checks its contents (`tools/dev/check-dist.js`); the zip is kept for a week as an artifact.
- `npm run dev` (DevTools, reload when `src/` changes, restart when `electron/` changes), `npm start -- --profile=empty|sample|real`, and `npm run sample`, a made-up library of about 300 songs with covers, playlists and favorites to develop against (`tools/dev/start.js`, `tools/dev/make-sample-library.js`, `electron/dev-tools.js`).
- Run from Source with `npm start` (own dev data folder `.dev-data/`), and `npm run dist` for a portable folder (`dist/Xplayer`, `portable.txt` beside the exe keeps the library and settings in `Xplayer-Data`). `tools/dev/import-library.js` copies an existing library in. See `electron/app-paths.js`.
- New interface: one custom scrollbar everywhere (slim thumb in the theme color, no arrow buttons). Settings > Appearance > Scrollbar color switches it to a neutral grey, and Scrollbar shape picks a flush bar or a wall tab.
- Two interfaces ship together. Switch in Settings > Interface (either interface); the choice is saved. Flags `--ui=next`, `--ui=current` and `XPLAYER_UI` still decide the first load.
- New interface: startup view, notification center with Cancel, tooltips, accent themes, density, resizable library panel, lyrics, metadata editor, settings sections, dialogs, search, library views.
- Cancel for downloads and album art search. The download's partial file is removed; the cover search stops after the current batch.

### Changed
- Agent folder: the finished dev plan, the visualizer plan and the stable-sizes and tooltip tables moved to `.agent/archive/` (see its `README.md`); what the dev plan left open is in `.agent/PARKED.md`.
- Python is no longer used by the app: tags are read and written only by the built-in Node writer, and `electron/metadata-editor.py` and the `XPLAYER_TAG_BACKEND` switch are gone. WMA, APE and WV files can be read but their tags cannot be edited (the editor says so); Python could not edit them either, only replace their covers.
- Both interfaces now carry a Content-Security-Policy: scripts only from the app's own files (no inline script, no eval), no requests from the page, `https:` only for media. Ten inline click handlers (the two fixed rows of the left panel, the import dialog, the folder list, the track information close button) now use `data-action`, and the new interface's inline start-up script is a file (`ui-next/startup/mount-legacy-dom.js`).
- Tools are in folders (`tools/checks`, `tools/dev`, `tools/fonts`, `tools/icons`); one `tools/fonts/fetch-fonts.js` replaces the four font downloaders; `npm run smoke` starts the real app and checks the basics; new `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`; the docs no longer describe the App folder as the place the app runs from.
- The app starts in the new interface; the old one is a switch in Settings > Interface (or `--ui=current`).
- Pages load from `src/` and the library from the data folder; covers are served from the library's covers folder, so code and data are separate. Old App folders keep working (a `MusicPlayerOutput` next to the code, or `MUSIC_PLAYER_OUTPUT_DIR`).
- Polish 1 plan files are archived with `tools/dev/archive-polish-docs.js`; `.agent/E-D-REMAINING.md` keeps what agents D and E still have to do.
- Library panel: switching the filter chips or the A to Z / Recent order fades the rows in.
- Removing a queue song that is not playing no longer redraws the song list; a pin change draws the left panel once.
- Space after a mouse click goes to the page instead of repeating the click.
- Compact density also shortens library panel rows.
- A volume change during a track's first second is kept (fade-in no longer overwrites it).

### Fixed
- Multi tag finder: a release that names the song differently from the file is now a result ("none" with the reason), not an error, so a run of such songs no longer stops as if the connection were down. A title with a remaster, mono or deluxe note ("In My Life (Remastered 2009)") is searched again without the note when nothing is found, and the finder looks through 25 recordings per song instead of 8, so famous songs are not left with only compilations.
- The undo history (AI plans and tag changes): when it cannot be written (a read-only folder, a full disk), an AI plan that already ran no longer ends in an error with a dead Undo button. The change is reported, Undo works until the app is closed, and the notification says so. A finished undo that cannot be written down is no longer reported as an error.
- Settings sliders (Crossfade, Fade in and out, ReplayGain trim, equalizer): the thumb now reaches both ends of the track. It stopped about 5 px short of each end because its hover halo was drawn inside the thumb's box; the halo is now a shadow around the dot, with room for it at the ends.
- The logo (application menu) button no longer pops in alone at the top left while the app starts; it appears with the rest of the title bar.
- The tooltip no longer flashes an accent (yellow) border and ring when it opens; it fades in and slides 3 px as before.
- The right panel's lyrics scrollbar now runs from the header's line to the bottom of the panel (the strips above and under it are gone), and the left panel's library list has room under its last row while its scroll thumb is unchanged.
- Changing or reading a cover with the built-in tag writer now works (it never did: its own check failed and the work fell back to Python). An MP3 no longer gets a stale ID3v1 tag added when it is saved.
- New interface: dragging playlists, folders and songs shows the drop line, target ring, folder tint and drag label again, and dropping songs on the Library panel adds them to Liked Songs.
- Startup: the startup view shows from the first paint instead of after the page loaded.
- Adapter API tests load their modules under the `<base href>` used by the new interface.
